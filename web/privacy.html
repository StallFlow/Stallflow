Privacy policy

StallFlow · last updated 20 October 2026

StallFlow is software that market stalls in South Africa use to take pre-orders, ring up sales and keep track of stock. Each stall's records are kept in a Google Sheet belonging to StallFlow and shared with that stall's owner. This page explains what is kept, why, and for how long, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

If you order from a stall

  • We keep your name, phone number, what you ordered and any note you add, so the stall can hand your order over on market day.
  • We only keep these once you tick the consent box on the order page, and we record when you did.
  • Your name and number are deleted after the market. The record of what was sold stays, without your details, so the stall can see its numbers.
  • We do not use your details for marketing and do not share them with anyone except the stall you ordered from.

If you work at a stall

  • You sign in with your Google account. From Google we receive only your email address, name and confirmation that the email is yours. We never see your Google password, and we cannot read your email, files or anything else in your Google account.
  • We keep your email, name, role and when you last signed in, so the stall owner can control who has access.
  • Changes you make in the app (sales, stock and price changes) are recorded with your email, so the stall has an accurate history.
  • When the owner removes you, you can no longer sign in. Ask them, or us, if you want your details deleted.

Where it is kept

In Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, run by Google, and in your phone's own storage so the app works without signal. Signing out clears your sign-in from the phone.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact the stall you dealt with, or StallFlow through the stall. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
